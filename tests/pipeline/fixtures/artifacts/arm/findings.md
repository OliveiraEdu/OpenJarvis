## ARM Processors (CPU) Use on Servers (2025-2030)

### Facts Collected

1. **Market Size and Trends**:
   - The global ARM server chip market was valued at approximately $3.1 billion in 2023 and is projected to grow at a CAGR of 18.3% from 2025 to 2030, reaching $8.9 billion by 2030. This growth is attributed to the increasing adoption of ARM servers in data centers due to their lower energy costs and technological advancements. (Source: ResearchAndMarkets, 2023)
   - URL: https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6005835/arm-server-chip-global-market-insights

2. **Market Share**:
   - Arm-based processors achieved a 25% market share in Q2 2025, up from 15% a year earlier. This growth is driven by deployments in AI-centric data centers, with Nvidia’s Grace CPUs and rack-scale Blackwell platforms acting as major catalysts for Arm adoption in cloud infrastructure. (Source: LinkedIn, Carlos Unda, 2025)
   - URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/carlosunda_interesting-technology-innovation-activity-7374090192443494400-duQS

3. **Market Size and Growth**:
   - The global ARM-based servers market generated USD 7.5 billion in 2025 and is predicted to grow to USD 32.7 billion by 2035, recording a CAGR of 15.80%. (Source: Market.us, 2025)
   - URL: https://market.us/report/arm-based-servers-market

4. **North America Market Share**:
   - North America held a dominant market position in 2025, capturing more than a 42% share with USD 3.16 billion in revenue. (Source: Market.us, 2025)
   - URL: https://market.us/report/arm-based-servers-market

5. **Google Cloud Adoption**:
   - Google Cloud announced C4A metal as its first bare metal instance running on Axion processors in November 2025, supporting enterprise use cases requiring physical server control inside cloud infrastructure. (Source: FutureMarketInsights, 2025)
