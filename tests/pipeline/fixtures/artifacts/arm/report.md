# ARM Processors (CPU) Use on Servers (2025-2030)

## Introduction

This report provides an overview of the use of ARM processors in servers globally from 2025 to 2030. The focus is on market trends, growth projections, and key players driving the adoption of ARM-based technology in the server industry. The findings are based on data collected from various sources and are intended to serve as a foundational document for further analysis and strategic planning.


## Executive Summary

ARM processors are increasingly being adopted in the server market due to their energy efficiency and performance capabilities. The global ARM server chip market is projected to grow significantly, with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 18.3% from 2025 to 2030, reaching a value of $8.9 billion by 2030. This growth is driven by the increasing demand for energy-efficient solutions in data centers and cloud infrastructure.

The market share of ARM-based processors is expected to rise, with a projected 25% market share in Q2 2025, up from 15% a year earlier. This growth is attributed to the deployment of ARM processors in AI-centric data centers, with major catalysts such as Nvidia’s Grace CPUs and rack-scale Blackwell platforms.

The global ARM-based servers market is anticipated to grow from USD 7.5 billion in 2025 to USD 32.7 billion by 2035, recording a CAGR of 15.80%. North America is expected to hold a dominant market position, capturing more than a 42% share with USD 3.16 billion in revenue in 2025. Google Cloud's adoption of ARM-based processors, including the C4A metal instance running on Axion processors, is also a significant factor in the growth of the ARM server market.


## Detailed Analysis

### Market Trends and Growth

The global ARM server chip market was valued at approximately $3.1 billion in 2023 and is projected to grow at a CAGR of 18.3% from 2025 to 2030, reaching $8.9 billion by 2030. This growth is attributed to the increasing adoption of ARM servers in data centers due to their lower energy costs and technological advancements. The market is expected to see significant growth as more organizations adopt ARM-based solutions for their energy efficiency and performance capabilities.

### Market Share

Arm-based processors achieved a 25% market share in Q2 2025, up from 15% a year earlier. This growth is driven by deployments in AI-centric data centers, with Nvidia’s Grace CPUs and rack-scale Blackwell platforms acting as major catalysts for Arm adoption in cloud infrastructure. The increasing demand for energy-efficient solutions in data centers and cloud infrastructure is a key factor contributing to this growth.

### Market Size and Growth

The global ARM-based servers market generated USD 7.5 billion in 2025 and is predicted to grow to USD 32.7 billion by 2035, recording a CAGR of 15.80%. This growth is expected to be driven by the increasing demand for ARM-based solutions in various sectors, including cloud computing, data centers, and enterprise applications.

### North America Market Share

North America held a dominant market position in 2025, capturing more than a 42% share with USD 3.16 billion in revenue. The region is expected to continue leading the market due to its strong presence in the technology and cloud computing sectors. The increasing adoption of ARM-based solutions in data centers and cloud infrastructure is a key driver of this growth.

### Google Cloud Adoption

Google Cloud announced C4A metal as its first bare metal instance running on Axion processors in November 2025, supporting enterprise use cases requiring physical server control inside cloud infrastructure. This adoption is a significant milestone for the ARM-based server market, as it demonstrates the growing acceptance of ARM-based solutions in the cloud computing sector.

### CAGR by Country

The United States is expected to have a CAGR of 12.8% in the ARM-based servers market. This growth is attributed to the increasing demand for energy-efficient solutions in data centers and cloud infrastructure. The United States is expected to continue leading the market due to its strong presence in the technology and cloud computing sectors.
## Conclusions

ARM processors are increasingly being adopted in the server market due to their energy efficiency, scalability, and performance. While they are still less prevalent than x86 architectures in traditional server environments, the trend is shifting towards ARM-based servers, especially in cloud computing and edge computing scenarios. The growth of ARM processors in the server market is expected to continue, driven by advancements in chip technology and the demand for more efficient computing solutions.

The adoption of ARM processors in servers is also influenced by the growing need for energy-efficient and cost-effective solutions. As the technology matures and more vendors enter the market, ARM-based servers are likely to become a significant part of the server landscape. However, challenges such as software compatibility and the need for specialized hardware support remain, which could slow down the adoption rate.

Overall, the future of ARM processors in the server market is promising, with potential for significant growth and innovation.
